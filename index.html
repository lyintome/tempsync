03 The Science

Built on real sleep science.

Our blanket triggers the body temperature changes your circadian rhythm already needs — at exactly the right time.

  • i

    Melatonin rises at night

    Your body prepares for sleep as core temperature drops.

  • ii

    Cortisol rises at dawn

    Body temperature lifts to bring you out of deep sleep naturally.

  • iii

    Temperature is the conductor

    Cooler bodies sleep deeper. Gradual warming reduces sleep inertia — the groggy feeling on wake.
